What I Do

Public health, practiced close to the community.

I design the programs, messages, and mentorship pipelines that turn public health science into services people actually use.

Program & Access Design

Built Hawai’i's first PrEP navigation pathway and served as an HIV/STI Disease Intervention Specialist — turning biomedical prevention tools into services people can actually reach.

Health Communication

Designed bilingual sexual health campaigns, outbreak communication plans, and public health storytelling that meet communities in the digital spaces where they already are.

Leadership & Mentorship

President of two student organizations at Columbia, NMAC NextGen Fellowship mentor to 30 emerging HIV advocates, and a Sexual Health Educator through Project STAY.
