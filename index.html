What I Do

Public health, practiced close to the community.

I design the programs, analyses, and learning experiences that turn public health science into services people actually use. It's work I've never done alone, but alongside clinicians, community organizers, and mentors who've shaped how I lead.

Public Health Leadership

Co-built Hawai’i's first PrEP navigation pathway, managed $1.5M in government contracts, and coordinated outbreak response training with state health departments and the CDC.

Research & Data

Nearly a decade of HIV and public health research, from NIH-funded international training to managing a statewide analysis of 8,000+ respondents across 80 outcome measures in Tableau.

Education & Mentorship

Trained 100+ first responders, taught 40 graduate students as a Teaching Assistant, mentored 20+ emerging HIV leaders nationally, and teach yoga in my community.

Featured Work

Public health leadership, in practice.

Program Design

Hawai’i's First PrEP Navigation Program

Led the build-out of a full navigation pathway in partnership with clinical and outreach staff at the Hawai’i Health & Harm Reduction Center, covering outreach, screening, insurance and co-pay assistance, prescribing, and quarterly follow-up. Exceeded year-one enrollment targets by 300%.
